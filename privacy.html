SHARK™ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Welcome to SHARK™. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how SHARK™ may collect, use, store, and process information when you use our Discord bot and its services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, SHARK™ may process:

  • Discord User IDs.
  • Discord Server IDs.
  • Channel and Role IDs.
  • Moderation records, such as warnings and actions.
  • Server configuration and bot settings.
  • Giveaway participation information.
  • Security and activity logs.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide moderation and administration features.
  • Protect servers through Anti-Nuke and Security systems.
  • Maintain server settings and configurations.
  • Provide logging and utility features.
  • Manage giveaways and participation.
  • Improve bot functionality and reliability.

3. Data Storage

Information may be stored in the bot's database or local storage systems when required for its features. The type and duration of storage depend on the information and the feature being used.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the bot, comply with legal requirements, or protect the service and its users.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the bot's features and maintain its systems. For data-related requests, please contact our support team.

7. Third-Party Services

SHARK™ operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

8. Children's Privacy

SHARK™ is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Users should follow Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any updates will be published on this page.
